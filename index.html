BACHELOR'S THESIS SEP 2025 – JUN 2026

Multi-objective optimization
for the blood supply chain

For my bachelor's thesis, I developed a multi-objective location-distribution model for a blood supply chain. The model uses the effective capacity of blood donation centers to account for blood perishability.

  • I used an exponential distribution to estimate the effective supply capacity of donation centers. The model allows for blood shortages.
  • I solved the model using epsilon-constraint and weighted-sum methods.

Supervised by Prof. Kourosh Eshghi
Sharif University of Technology
